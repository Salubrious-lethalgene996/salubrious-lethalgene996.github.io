## ⚙️ Using the sensitivity calculator

The calculator simplifies the way you handle sensitivity. Follow these steps to find your ideal settings.

1.  Open the application from your desktop icon.
2.  Select your target game from the list.
3.  Enter your current mouse dots per inch (DPI) setting.
4.  Enter your current in-game sensitivity value.
5.  Click the Calculate button.

The tool displays your sensitivity in units of distance per 360-degree turn. This measurement remains the same across different games. If you want to use the same feel in another game, select that game from the conversion menu. The tool updates the value for you.

## 📊 Analyzing your aim

The analysis section provides feedback on your mouse movement. This data helps you identify habits that limit your performance.

*   **Steady hand tracking:** The tool measures how well you follow a moving target.
*   **Flick accuracy:** The tool logs how close your crosshair lands to a target after a quick movement.
*   **Correction patterns:** The tool identifies if you consistently overshoot or undershoot targets.

To use this feature, select an aim scenario. Move your mouse to track the targets on the screen. The tool tracks your performance in real time. Once the test finishes, view your score. Look at the recommendation tab for advice on adjusting your sensitivity based on your results.
